Preparing for the CCNA can feel overwhelming because the syllabus is broad.
The trick is not to learn everything at once, but to build a **layered mental
model** and to *practise* it as you go.

## The three pillars

1. **Theory** — understand *why* before *how*. Read a lesson, then explain it
   out loud in your own words.
2. **Practice** — configure it. Use the [labs](/labs/) to type the commands
   until they feel automatic.
3. **Recall** — test yourself with [quizzes](/quizzes/) and
   [interview questions](/interview-questions/). Retrieval beats re-reading.

## A four-week rhythm

| Week | Focus | Deliverable |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | OSI model, TCP/IP, addressing | Explain the layers from memory |
| 2 | Switching and VLANs | Build a two-switch topology in the sandbox |
| 3 | Routing fundamentals | Read a routing table out loud |
| 4 | Security, services and review | Score 80%+ on every quiz |

## Make it stick

- Study a little every day — streaks matter more than marathon sessions.
- After each lab, close the terminal and write the commands from memory.
- When you get a question wrong, read the **explanation** and move on. Do not
  memorise the answer; understand the reason.

> Consistency compounds. Twenty focused minutes a day beats a five-hour weekend
> once a month.
